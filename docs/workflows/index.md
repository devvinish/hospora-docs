# Workflows

!!! info "This chapter is being written"
    Each workflow follows one patient from the first screen to the last, with links to the chapters of the
    screens it uses:

    - **An O.P.D. visit**: registration, appointment and token, consultation and prescription, tests, the
      bill, medicines.
    - **A lab test**: the order, the sample and its barcode, results, verification, and the printed report
      sent on WhatsApp.
    - **From admission to discharge**: admission, advance, ward chart, operation, discharge summary, I.P.
      final bill, dues.
    - **A cashless (TPA) admission**: pre-authorisation, the claim, settlement, TDS and write-off.
    - **The end of the day**: collections by mode, day-end closing, the day book, the export to the
      accounts.

# First-time Setup

Before the first patient, an administrator fills in the settings below, in this order. Each links to its
page in this guide.

| # | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Administration > Hospital Details](../administration/settings.md#hospital-details) | the hospital's name, address and phones (printed on every document), the bill number prefix, the GSTIN and state code, the **UPI ID** for payment QR codes, and the **default district and state** of patients |
| 2 | [Administration > Roles](../administration/users.md#roles) | the menus of each role, and the largest discount each may give |
| 3 | [Administration > Application Users](../administration/users.md#application-users) | a user for everyone, with a role and a password. First give **yourself** the Administrator role and a password, to leave set-up mode. |
| 4 | [Administration > Taxes](../administration/settings.md#taxes) | the GST on medicines and on services, if any |
| 5 | [Masters > Units](../masters/tariff.md#units) and [Doctors](../masters/tariff.md#doctors) | the O.P.D. departments with their rooms, and the doctors with their qualification and registration number |
| 6 | [Masters > OP Charge Items](../masters/tariff.md#op-charge-items) | the registration fee |
| 7 | [Masters > Cash Heads (Tariff)](../masters/tariff.md#cash-groups-subgroups-and-heads-the-tariff) | every service with its rate |
| 8 | [Masters > I.P. Wards](../masters/tariff.md#ip-wards) and [I.P. Bill Items](../masters/tariff.md#ip-bill-items) | the wards with their beds and daily charges, and the charges of the I.P. bill |
| 9 | [Masters > Empanelled Companies](../masters/tariff.md#empanelled-companies) | the insurers, TPAs, companies and schemes, with their GSTIN |
| 10 | [Appointments > Doctor Schedules](../appointments/schedules.md) | the clinic days and hours of each doctor |
| 11 | [Investigation > Lab Test Settings](../investigation/lab-settings.md) and [Normal Ranges](../investigation/lab-settings.md#normal-ranges) | the specimen and tube of each test, and the ranges by age and sex |
| 12 | [Pharmacy > Medicines](../pharmacy/stock.md#medicines) | the medicines kept, added from the [catalogue](../pharmacy/stock.md#medicine-catalogue), and the opening stock of each batch |
| 13 | [Finance > Packages and Schemes](../finance/insurance.md#packages-and-schemes), [Referrers](../finance/shares.md#referrers), [Share Rules](../finance/shares.md#share-rules) | only if the hospital uses them |
| 14 | [Finance > Ledger Settings](../finance/gst-accounts.md#ledger-settings) | the accountant's ledgers, if the accounts are exported to Tally or Zoho Books |
| 15 | [Administration > Message Templates](../administration/settings.md#message-templates) | the words of the WhatsApp messages |
| 16 | [Clinical > Form Templates](../clinical/forms.md#form-templates) | the hospital's own consent forms and letters, if they differ |

!!! tip "Test before you start"
    Register a test patient, bill a test, sell a medicine and print each document. Check the letterhead,
    the numbers and the taxes, then cancel the test documents.
